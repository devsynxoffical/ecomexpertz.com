## 1. Platform Overview & Architecture

**Ecomxstocks** is an e-commerce performance marketing and merchant optimization platform. The system connects high-volume e-commerce merchants (operating on eBay, Walmart, and Temu) with remote members who optimize listing ratings, improve organic search placement, and validate inventory transactions.

### Key Highlights:
- **Zero Complex Installation**: Fully responsive web platform working across iPhones, Android, tablets, and desktops.
- **USDT TRC20 Settlements**: Fast, low-fee cryptocurrency deposits and payouts.
- **Dynamic Tiering**: Progressive VIP levels unlocking higher earning yields.
- **Full Operational Admin Control**: Complete transparency over users, tasks, deposit confirmations, ledger entries, and live support channels.

## 2. Member (End-User) Experience

### 2.1 Registration and Authentication
1. Go to **[https://ecomxstocks.com/](https://ecomxstocks.com/)**.
2. Click **"Sign Up"** or **"Start Today"**.
3. Fill in:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 6 characters)
   - Security PIN (used for authorizing payout withdrawals)
4. Once registered, members are automatically logged in and land on the storefront/dashboard.

---

### 2.2 Member Dashboard & Tier Levels
The member dashboard provides a clear overview of:
- **Available Balance ($ USDT)**: Total funds available for task matching and withdrawal.
- **Today's Profit ($ USDT)**: Accumulated commissions earned during the current 24-hour cycle.
- **Completed Tasks**: Progress bar showing completed tasks out of the daily package (e.g., `14 / 25 Tasks Completed`).
- **Account Tier**:
  | Tier Level | Balance Requirement | Platform | Commission Rate |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | **Tier 1 (Silver)** | $0 - $499 USDT | eBay | **4%** per task |
  | **Tier 2 (Gold)** | $500 - $899 USDT | Walmart | **8%** per task |
  | **Tier 3 (Platinum)** | $900+ USDT | Temu | **12%** per task |

---

### 2.3 Completing Tasks & Earning Commissions
1. On the Dashboard, the member clicks **"Start Tasks"** or **"Grab Next Order"**.
2. The system loads an active merchant product (featuring product image, retail price, seller name, and estimated commission).
3. The member reviews the product details and clicks **"Submit Rating / Optimize Order"**.
4. The system executes a quick verification animation (validating merchant listing signals).
5. Once complete:
   - The product value and earned commission are credited instantly to the member's wallet balance.
   - The progress counter increments (e.g., from `14/25` to `15/25`).
   - The member can proceed directly to the next task.

---

### 2.4 Understanding the Learning Steps (1 to 25)
For new accounts or accounts placed on the training curriculum:
- The system includes a progressive sequence of 25 structured steps.
- **Step 1** introduces the member to initial verification with an entry-level deposit requirement ($10).
- Intermediate steps gradually build familiarity with marketplace orders and yield calculations.
- **Step 25 (Graduation Milestone)** signifies the completion of onboarding and transitions the member to standard high-volume merchant allocations.

---

### 2.5 The Deposit Requirement (Hurdle / Security Barrier)
During task progression, if a task requires a balance greater than the member's current wallet funds:
1. The system alerts the member: **"Required Deposit Barrier: Deposit $X to Continue"**.
2. This ensures member commitment for high-ticket merchant inventory orders.
3. Once the member deposits the required difference:
   - The task unlocks immediately.
   - The member completes the task.
   - **Both the deposit amount AND the high commission are credited right back into the member's withdrawable balance**.

---

### 2.6 How Members Submit Deposits (USDT TRC20)
1. On the top navigation bar or wallet modal, click **"Deposit"**.
2. The deposit window displays:
   - Platform official **USDT TRC20 Deposit Address**.
   - Scannable **QR Code** for Binance, OKX, Trust Wallet, Bitget, or Bybit.
3. The member sends the desired USDT amount via their crypto wallet/exchange using the **TRC20 (Tron)** network.
4. The member enters the **Deposit Amount ($)** and pastes the **Transaction Hash (TxID)**.
5. Clicks **"Submit Deposit For Verification"**.
6. The request moves to `Pending` status. Once verified by the Admin (typically 2 to 5 minutes), the funds reflect in the member's balance.

---

### 2.7 Requesting a New Task Package
When a member finishes all 25 daily tasks:
1. The system displays: **"Daily Quota Complete (25/25)"**.
2. The member can click **"Request New Task Package"**.
3. A notification flag `️ [PENDING REQUEST]` appears in the Admin Portal.
4. The operations admin reviews the account and issues a fresh 25-task package or specialized VIP package.

---

### 2.8 Withdrawing Earnings to USDT TRC20
1. Click **"Withdraw"** from the dashboard.
2. Enter:
   - **Withdrawal Amount ($ USDT)**.
   - **Destination USDT TRC20 Wallet Address**.
   - **Security PIN / Password**.
3. Click **"Confirm Withdrawal Request"**.
4. The request is submitted to the Admin operations team for payout processing.

---

### 2.9 Live Chat & Telegram Support
- Members can click the floating **Chat Widget** in the bottom right corner anytime.
- Official Telegram support channels and priority helpdesk links are accessible directly from the header, footer, and support drawer.

## 3. Administrator Operations Manual

The Admin Portal provides complete oversight and real-time execution capabilities.

### 3.1 Accessing the Admin Portal
- **Direct URL**: `https://ecomxstocks.com/?admin=1` *(or `https://ecomxstocks.com/admin`)*
- **Master Admin Credentials**:
  - **Email**: `admin@devsynx.com`
  - **Password**: `admin123`
- Click **"Sign In to Operations Control"**.

---

### 3.2 Dashboard & Financial KPI Tracking
Upon signing in, the Admin Header and KPI cards display:
- **Total Registered Members**: Live count of client accounts.
- **Total Vault Balance**: Aggregate USDT held across all user accounts.
- **Pending Deposits**: Number of submitted member deposits awaiting on-chain confirmation.
- **Pending Withdrawals**: Number of payout requests awaiting release.
- **Active Task Catalogue**: Total number of tasks active in the marketplace pool.

---

### 3.3 Member Management & Balance Control
Navigate to the **"Users & Ledgers"** tab:
1. **Search & Filter**: Find any user by name or email.
2. **Privacy Protection**: Member emails are masked by default (e.g., `ha***n@g***.com`). Click **"️ Unmask Emails"** when verifying customer records.
3. **Inspect Account**:
   - View exact current balance.
   - View assigned VIP tier.
   - View total tasks completed.
4. **Action Buttons per User**:
   - **"Manual Deposit"**: Directly credit any amount to the user's wallet without requiring a TxID.
   - **"Assign Tasks"**: Open the modal to assign 25 random tasks or pick a specific task from the catalogue.
   - **"Reset Password"**: Set a new temporary password for the client.
   - **"Suspend / Activate"**: Toggle account status.

---

### 3.4 Approving & Rejecting Deposit Requests
Navigate to the **"Deposits"** tab:
1. You will see all deposits submitted by members with:
   - Member Name & Email
   - Amount ($ USDT)
   - Submitted Transaction Hash (TxID) with direct TronScan lookup link
   - Timestamp
2. **To Approve**:
   - Click **" Approve Deposit"**.
   - The user's account balance is **instantly credited** with the deposit amount.
   - An immutable transaction record is logged in the Financial Ledger.
3. **To Reject**:
   - Click **" Reject"**.
   - The deposit status updates to Rejected; no funds are added.

---

### 3.5 Direct Manual Balance Credit
If an admin needs to credit a user's wallet directly (e.g. bonus, correction, offline cash transfer):
1. Navigate to **"Finance & Manual Credit"**.
2. Select the target user from the dropdown.
3. Enter the credit amount (e.g., `$150.00`).
4. Enter an internal reference note (e.g., `Promo Bonus` or `VIP Upgrade`).
5. Click **"Credit User Wallet"**.
6. The user's balance updates immediately in real-time.

---

### 3.6 Approving & Processing Withdrawals
Navigate to the **"Withdrawals"** tab:
1. Review pending payout requests:
   - Member Name & Balance
   - Requested Payout Amount
   - Member's USDT TRC20 Wallet Address
2. Once the admin transfers the funds on-chain:
   - Click **" Mark as Paid / Approved"**.
   - The withdrawal is marked complete and deducted permanently.
3. If details are invalid:
   - Click **" Reject & Refund Balance"**. The funds return to the user's available balance.

---

### 3.7 Task & Catalogue Management (Full Controls)

The Task Management section is located under the **"Task Catalogue"** tab. This is where operations configure everything members can complete.

#### A. Creating a New Task
1. Click the green **"+ Add New Task"** button.
2. Fill out the task details:
   - **Product Title**: Name of the item (e.g., `Anker Wireless Noise Cancelling Headphones`).
   - **Platform**: Select `eBay`, `Walmart`, or `Temu`.
   - **Product Price ($)**: The retail price (e.g., `$89.99`).
   - **Commission Rate (%)**: Earning yield for the user (e.g., `6%`).
   - **Required Deposit Barrier ($)**: Set a minimum deposit amount needed if you wish to gate this task (or `$0` for open access).
   - **Image URL**: Enter any high-resolution image URL (Unsplash or direct CDN).
   - **Learning Milestone (Optional)**: Toggle ON if this is part of the 1 to 25 onboarding sequence, and select the step number (`Step 1` through `Step 25`).
3. Click **"Save & Add to Catalogue"**.

#### B. Modifying Existing Tasks
1. In the Task Catalogue table, locate the task.
2. Click the bright blue **"Edit"** button.
3. In the modal, modify:
   - Price, Commission Rate, or Deposit Requirement.
   - Status (Active vs. Disabled / Paused).
4. Click **"Save Task Changes"**.

#### C. Assigning Tasks (Bulk 25 Random vs. Specific Tasks)
Admins have two powerful assignment tools:
- **Option 1: Quick 25 Random Task Assignment**
  - Click **" Quick Assign 25 Random Tasks"**.
  - Select the target user.
  - The system automatically shuffles the active product pool and assigns a balanced 25-task package to the user's dashboard.
- **Option 2: Assign Specific Task**
  - Next to any individual product in the catalogue, click **"Assign"**.
  - Choose the target client from the dropdown.
  - Click **"Confirm & Assign Task"**. This specific product will now appear next in the user's task queue.

#### D. Configuring Learning Milestone Steps (1 to 25)
- Tasks marked with `isLearning: true` are tied to steps 1 through 25.
- You can inspect and adjust each step's required deposit:
  - **Step 1**: Initial tutorial milestone (Standard: $10 deposit barrier).
  - **Steps 2 to 24**: Progressive difficulty and higher product prices.
  - **Step 25**: Graduation milestone (Standard: $300 deposit barrier).

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: Why does a user see a "Deposit Required" screen when grabbing a task?
**Answer**: The user reached a task where the required product barrier exceeds their current balance. Once the user deposits the difference via USDT TRC20 and the admin approves it, the task unlocks and the full deposit + commission return to their balance.

#### Q2: What network is supported for cryptocurrency transfers?
**Answer**: **USDT on TRC20 (Tron Network)**. It provides near-instant finality and minimal gas fees (typically ~$1).

#### Q3: How do I assign a new set of 25 tasks once a user finishes?
**Answer**: Go to **Admin Portal &rarr; Users & Ledgers**, locate the user row (which will display `️ [PENDING REQUEST]`), click **"Assign Task"**, and click **" Quick Assign 25 Random Tasks Now"**.

#### Q4: Can I change a task's commission rate or price anytime?
**Answer**: Yes. Go to **Admin Portal &rarr; Task Catalogue**, find the task, click **"Edit"**, update the numbers, and click **"Save Task Changes"**.

#### Q5: Can users withdraw their balance immediately after completing tasks?
**Answer**: Yes. Once their 25-task package is completed and the account is in good standing, they can submit a withdrawal request for admin approval.
